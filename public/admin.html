Create district rate-admin (licensed district head)

Each district has ONE head — a government officer (for example the District Food Controller) or the local person the master appoints for that district. Before creating the account, check the officer's appointment document yourself (appointment letter, posting / transfer order, service card, Market Committee authorisation or CNIC) and record it here — officers hold no commercial licence, so there is no licence number. There is no automatic government check: no public system verifies a government appointment, and this site never claims to verify one. The account starts INACTIVE — activate it below, and the district becomes officially managed: only that licensed admin can then enter its rates, and the master cannot enter rates directly for it. Suspend ends the login immediately. The officer's name and document numbers are shown only here, never on the public site — the public sees only the designation and district.
