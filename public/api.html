Public live API

Free JSON, no key needed, no sign-up. Every rate carries its source and date — nothing is estimated. This API serves this app's own database; there is no official government API for mandi rates, and this does not claim to be one.

EndpointWhat it returnsExample
GET /healthPlatform health check — {"ok":true}, no database or login needed/health
GET /api/liveLatest snapshot date, province/district counts, districts with rates/api/live
GET /api/provincesAll province/territory groups with district counts/api/provinces
GET /api/districts?province=…Districts of one province, each with a has_rates flag/api/districts?province=Punjab
GET /api/metaCities, provinces, items, latest date, sources/api/meta
GET /api/today?city=…&category=…Latest rates for one district, with day changes and verify flags. category is vegetables, fruits or other — rows carry category and the Urdu unit (unit_ur: کلو / درجن). If the district has no rates yet, it returns its province hub's latest rates instead, with nearest_rates_from and nearest_rates_date set — never relabelled as the district's own. If the district has an active licensed head, the response also carries official: {designation, district} — the public "Officially managed by" line (officer name and licence number are never public)/api/today?city=Lahore&category=fruits
POST /api/chatRate chat: body {"q":"tomato rate in Lahore?"} (English or Urdu). Answers only from the rates on file — rate, Urdu unit, date, source — or says plainly that no rate was found. Never invents a price; a fallback rate is always labelled with its real district—
GET /api/movers?city=…&limit=5Top risers and fallers for one district/api/movers?city=Lahore
GET /api/history?item=…&city=…Price history points for one item in one district/api/history?item=Tomato&city=Lahore
GET /api/cheapest?item=…Districts ranked by wholesale price for one item/api/cheapest?item=Tomato
POST /api/checkOvercharge verdict: body {"city","item","price_paid"}—
POST /api/district-admin/loginDistrict-admin login: body {"username","password"} → bearer token (24h). Send the token as an Authorization: Bearer header afterwards — never in a URL. 5 failed logins lock the account for 15 minutes—
POST /api/district-admin/logoutEnds the session server-side (the token stops working immediately)—
POST /api/district-admin/change-passwordChange your own password: body {"old_password","new_password"} (min 8 chars); other sessions are ended—
GET /api/district/photos?district=…Public: the latest approved rates-page photo per place for one district (image + date + source label + "Official rates page" caption), plus a small history of the last few days' approved photos. Pending or rejected photos never appear here/api/district/photos?district=Lahore
GET /api/photos/file/…The photo file itself. Approved photos are public; a pending photo is reachable only by its district's head or the master. Licence scans are never served here — they have their own authenticated endpoint—
POST /api/register/head / /api/register/managerPublic self-registration: body {"username","password","district","officer_name","designation","documents":[{"doc_type","doc_number","issuing_authority","issue_date","expiry_date"}]}. Document types: Appointment Letter, Posting-Transfer Order, Service Card, Market Committee Authorisation, CNIC, Other. The account stays PENDING until approved — a head is approved by the master, a manager by their district head. Pending accounts cannot log in—
POST /api/register/scanAttach a scan to one of your documents while your account is still pending: multipart form, fields username, doc_number, file scan (JPG/PNG/WebP image or PDF, max 5MB). The scan is visible only to your approver, never public—
GET /api/documents/scan/:idView one document scan — master, the account holder, or the head of the account's district only. Anyone else gets 403—
POST /api/district-admin/manager / /manager/activate / /manager/suspendHead-only (own district). Appoint a manager (body {"username","password","name","designation","appointment_ref","documents"} — the head's written order is mandatory), activate or suspend them. Suspended managers cannot log in and their live sessions end at once—
GET /api/district-admin/managersHead-only: the managers of your own district, with their documents and assigned places. Never another district's managers—
POST /api/district-admin/manager/placesHead-only: assign or change a manager's places (markets/areas) inside your own district — body {"username","places":["Lahore Mandi", …]}. A manager can propose rates and photos only for their assigned places—
GET /api/district-admin/pending-managersHead-only: manager registrations waiting for your approval, with their documents—
POST /api/district-admin/manager/approveHead-only: approve or reject a manager registration — body {"username","decision":"approve"|"reject","appointment_ref"}. Approving a manager requires recording the appointment order—
POST /api/district-manager/photoManager-only (token in the Authorization: Bearer header): upload a daily rates-page photo for one of your assigned places — multipart form, fields place, date (default today), source_label, file photo (JPG/PNG/WebP, max 5MB). The photo waits pending until your district head approves it; a new upload for the same place+date supersedes a still-pending one—
GET /api/district-admin/photosHead-only: your district's rates-page photos by status (?status=pending|approved|rejected|superseded)—
POST /api/photo/decideApprove or reject a rates-page photo — the district head (own district) or the master. Approving one supersedes any other approved photo for the same place+date—
GET /api/admin/pending-heads / /api/admin/pending-managersMaster-only: registrations waiting for master approval, with their documents—
POST /api/admin/head/approve / /api/admin/manager/approveMaster-only: approve or reject a registration — body {"username","decision":"approve"|"reject","appointment_ref"} (appointment order required for managers)—
POST /api/admin/district-manager / /api/admin/manager/places / /api/admin/district-managersMaster-only: create a manager for a district that has no head yet (bootstrap), set that manager's places, and list all managers—
POST /api/district-admin/rateDistrict head adds a rate for their own district only: body {"district","item","price","unit","source","date","kind","place"} (place is the market/area, optional). Any other district → 403. Wholesale prices are Rs per kg (stored as Rs/100kg, the AMIS convention) or Rs per dozen for dozen items. Managers cannot add rates directly — they propose through the review queue—
GET /api/review/queuePending proposed rates — master code via the X-Master-Code header, or a district-admin token (district admins see only their own district)—
POST /api/review/propose / /api/review/decidePropose a rate for review / approve or reject it (approve publishes it with its source and date). Wholesale prices in a proposal are Rs/100kg for kg items, Rs per dozen for dozen items—
GET /api/admin/recent / GET /api/admin/district-adminsMaster-only reads — the master code goes in the X-Master-Code header, never in a URL. The admin list shows each district admin's officer name, designation, appointment documents and active status—
POST /api/admin/district-admin / /update / /activate / /suspend / /removeMaster-only. Create / update / activate / suspend / remove a licensed district-head account. Creation requires officer_name, designation and a documents array (appointment letter, posting / transfer order, service card, Market Committee authorisation or CNIC — the documents are checked by the master against the paper record; there is no automatic government verification). Accounts start inactive; activating one makes its district officially managed, and suspending one blocks its login and ends all its sessions immediately. One head per district—
GET /api/admin/district-admin/auditMaster-only audit log: every district-admin action with the acting officer's designation and primary document reference—

Daily refresh: the admin panel pulls the public AMIS daily-changes page each day (wholesale only) and upserts it as a new dated snapshot. The AMIS ViewPrices database (~140 markets, all commodities) is refreshed the same way, at most once every 6 hours — values far outside the day's range are never auto-published; they wait in the review queue instead. Districts with no snapshot yet show their province hub's latest rates instead, clearly labelled with nearest_rates_from and nearest_rates_date — and only when the hub's rates are dated today or yesterday; otherwise they return empty rows, honestly — never estimated. Karachi retail is entered manually because its source page is not reliably parseable.
