District rate admin

District admins are created by the master admin (government officer or the local person running the district's rates). You can only add, edit or delete rates for your own district — every other district is blocked server-side. Each entry needs an item, a price, a unit, a source name and a date, and is shown to the public with that source and date.

Managers — my district

Food managers work under you: they enter rates for the places you assign them, and nothing they propose goes live until you approve it. A manager's credential is their appointment documents (appointment letter, posting / transfer order, service card, Market Committee authorisation or CNIC) — officers hold no commercial licence. Appoint a manager below, or approve a manager who registered themselves. Suspend ends their login immediately.
