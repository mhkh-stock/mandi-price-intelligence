        Overcharge checker — dukaandar ne zyada liya?

        Type what you paid. We compare it with the official bazaar rate on file and tell you plainly: fair, slightly above, or overcharged — and by how much. The verdict always names the city and date of the rate used; if your district has no official rate yet, it falls back to the official rate on file from another city and says so.

        Sources:

        Prices are published rates, not our own survey. Wholesale: AMIS Pakistan daily changes. Retail: Commissioner Karachi official list, plus the Business Recorder Peshawar survey (2 Nov 2026). If a rate looks wrong, the source and date above let you verify it.
