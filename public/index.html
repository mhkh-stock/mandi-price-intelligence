        Overcharge checker — dukaandar ne zyada liya?

        Type what you paid. We compare it with the official bazaar rate on file and tell you plainly: fair, slightly above, or overcharged — and by how much. The verdict always names the city and date of the rate used; if your district has no official rate yet, it falls back to the official rate on file from another city and says so.

        About Mandi Price Intelligence — اس ویب سائٹ کے بارے میں

        What it is: a public website that collects Pakistan's daily mandi (wholesale) and official retail rates for vegetables, fruit and chicken — for all 168 districts — and shows them in one simple table, with every rate's source and date. No estimates, no invented rates: a district with no published rate yet shows that plainly, or its province hub's latest rates, clearly labelled as nearest rates.

        How it works: 1) Pick your province, then your district. 2) Pick vegetables, fruit, or other. 3) Read the table — rates in Urdu units (کلو / درجن), with the date and source on every row. You can also ask the rate chat in English or Urdu, compare the cheapest city, check whether a shopkeeper overcharged you, or see the price trend.

        Where the rates come from: AMIS Pakistan (the government's daily wholesale changes and its wholesale price database), the Commissioner Karachi official retail list, the Business Recorder Peshawar survey, PBS weekly averages, the Daily Qudrat Islamabad list, and poultryrates.today — plus rates entered by each district's own rate-admin (a government officer or the local person running that district's rates), each labelled with its source and date. The morning search of district market committees' official pages is checked by a person and approved by an admin before anything shows — the site never guesses a price.

        Who runs it: M. Hashir — for corrections, a missing district, or a district-admin account, write to mhkh3200@gmail.com. This is a public information service, not a government website: the rates shown are the published rates of the sources named, and anyone can verify them from the source and date shown.
