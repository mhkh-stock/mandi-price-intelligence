Register as a district officer

District heads and food managers register here. Your account stays pending until it is approved — a head is approved by the master, a manager by the head of their own district. Only after approval can you log in and work. Registration asks for your appointment documents (appointment letter, posting / transfer order, service card, Market Committee authorisation or CNIC) — government officers do not hold a commercial licence, so there is no licence number to give. There is no automatic government check: the approver reads your documents and checks them against the paper record. Your documents and any scans you upload are visible only to the person who approves you — never to the public, never to another district.

Scans you upload live on this server's disk and are lost if the server is rebuilt — your recorded document details are kept in the account record. A re-upload after a rebuild restores the scan.

Appointment documents

At least one document is required. Add up to 5. A CNIC number must be exactly 13 digits. Dates cannot be in the future, and an expiry date cannot be before its issue date.

Upload a scan (optional, after registration)

You can attach a scan of one of your documents — an image (JPG, PNG, WebP) or a PDF, max 5MB. Scans can only be added while your account is still pending. Only your approver can view a scan.
